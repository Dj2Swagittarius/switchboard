Auto-dial

Before you run this: auto-dialing is for inventorying numbers you own or are authorized to test (finding which lines are a modem, fax, voice, or disconnected). In the US, dialing numbers you aren't authorized to call can violate the TCPA, state autodialer laws, and do-not-call / time-of-day rules. You are responsible for having the right to call every number in the list. Calls go out one at a time, paced, from your own phone line.
