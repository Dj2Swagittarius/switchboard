How these are stored

Encrypted on this PC with Windows' own protection, so only your Windows account can read them. Passwords are never shown again after you save them. To change one, type a new one; leave it blank to keep the saved one. Server addresses aren't secret and are kept in Switchboard's data folder on this PC.

Calls placed from this app are real calls on your line. Keep your provider's own app or a desk phone working as a backup, especially for 911, until you're confident in this one.
